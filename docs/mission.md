# Punk Systems

## The Problem

Every digital interaction - payments, logins, browsing, connectivity - all leave a permanent record. This data is retained, sold and repurposed without consent. Big tech and governments work together to control narratives and build a surveillance state where personal freedom and free political thought are captured and controlled by plutocracy. Democracy in darkness becomes dictatorship. Cash avoids tracking but financial institutions and governments are actively trying to suppress it and can't easily be used online. Privacy-preserving cryptography solves this, but the tools don't get to individuals who needs them as they are locked out by digital gatekeepers who shape their incentives through convenience. Digital privacy protection is our only solution.

## Mission Statement

> Make privacy the default across the digital stack by building zero-knowledge tools that leave no permanent record of who you are, what you buy, what you read, or where you connect.
